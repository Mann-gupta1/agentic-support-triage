# Account and identity policy

Identity verification requires a government-issued photo ID and a selfie check. Most verifications complete within minutes; manual review takes up to 2 working days.

A verification that fails twice must be escalated to the compliance team. Support agents must not advise the customer on how to pass a check, and must never ask the customer to send documents over chat or email.

A customer may close an account only when its balance is zero and no transfer is pending. Closure is permanent and the account number is not reissued.

Personal details such as legal name or date of birth can only be changed with supporting documentation. Address and phone number can be changed in the app without support involvement.

Never confirm or deny the existence of an account to anyone who has not passed authentication in the current session, regardless of what details they already appear to know.
