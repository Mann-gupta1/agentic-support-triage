# Card policy

A physical card ordered from the app arrives in 5 to 7 working days domestically and 10 to 14 working days internationally. Tracking appears in the app once the card leaves the issuing centre.

If a card has not arrived after 10 working days domestically, the agent may cancel the existing card and reissue a replacement at no charge. Only one free reissue is permitted per 90 days.

A card reported lost or stolen must be frozen immediately, before any other step. Freezing is reversible; a cancelled card is not, so never cancel on a first contact unless the customer explicitly confirms the card is gone for good.

A declined card transaction is most often caused by an expired card, insufficient balance, an unactivated card, or a country block. Check activation state before advising the customer to contact the merchant.

Card activation requires the customer to confirm the last four digits and complete one chip-and-PIN transaction. Contactless is disabled until that first chip transaction clears.
