# Transfer and payment policy

A domestic transfer between accounts on the platform settles instantly. A transfer to an external bank settles within one working day, and transfers submitted after 16:00 local time are processed the next working day.

An international transfer settles in 1 to 5 working days depending on the corridor and the intermediary bank. Fees are shown before confirmation and are never added afterwards.

A pending transfer can be cancelled by the customer from the app only while it is still pending. Once it has settled it cannot be reversed by support; the customer must raise a dispute instead.

A failed transfer returns the funds to the source account automatically within 3 working days. If the funds have not returned after 3 working days, escalate to the payments team with the transaction reference.

Transfers are subject to daily limits, which depend on account verification level. An unverified account is limited to a lower daily cap, and raising it requires identity verification, not a support override.
