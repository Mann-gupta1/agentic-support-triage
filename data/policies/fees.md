# Fees, exchange rates, and top-ups

The exchange rate shown at the time of a transaction is the rate applied. Rates move continuously, so a rate quoted earlier in a session is not held.

Top-ups by bank transfer are free. Top-ups by card may carry a fee charged by the card issuer, which is outside platform control and cannot be refunded by support.

A failed top-up usually reflects an issuer block rather than a platform decline. Advise the customer to contact their card issuer before retrying more than twice, because repeated retries can trigger a fraud hold.

Cash withdrawals are free up to a monthly allowance; beyond it a percentage fee applies. The allowance resets on the first day of each calendar month, not on the account anniversary.

Any fee charged in error may be refunded by an agent up to a small per-case limit. Above that limit the refund requires team-lead approval, and the agent must not promise the refund before approval is granted.
